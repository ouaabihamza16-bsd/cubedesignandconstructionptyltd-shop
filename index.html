About us

One company, from design to build

CUBE DESIGN AND CONSTRUCTION PTY LTD is an Australian proprietary company based in Mortdale, New South Wales, in Sydney's St George area. As the name says, our work brings building design and construction together.

The idea is simple. Rather than finding a designer and a builder separately and passing plans between them, you work with one company that looks after the project from the design stage through to construction.

The company was incorporated on 14 May 2009 (ACN 137 097 404). It has held ABN 62 137 097 404, and been registered for GST, since that date.

Services

Design and construction services

Our services follow the company name: designing a project, then building it. The descriptions below are general. Contact us to talk through what your project needs.

Design

Planning and design of your project before any building starts. We work through what you need from the space, how it should fit together and what is practical to build.

The scope of design work is agreed with you for each project.

Construction

Building work carried out by the company, following the plans agreed at the design stage. Because the same team worked on the design, questions on site go back to people who know the plans.
